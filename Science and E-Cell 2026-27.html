SCIENCE CLUB AND MATH CLUB

SCIENCE CLUB

Events of the Science Club are Rendezvous and Open Mic whose details are as given below:

Rendezvous: An invited speaker addresses the audience over a general topic (~1 hour) to direct a discussion afterwards. The post-session discussions often take over the blackboard, and never fail to provide a new perspective to look at the concepts involved. There are 6–8 Rendezvous sessions which will be held in the evenings.

Open Mic: The session brings together multiple speakers to deliver short, 3-minute talks. The students can also show scientific models/ perform experiments and demonstrations to express their ideas. It provides a common stage to both student and faculty members to interact over a variety of topics in one evening over refreshments. There are 6 Open Mic sessions during the year.

Apart from these, Science Club organizes informal science talks called ‘Random Talks’ where students join and discuss different scientific topics.

MATH CLUB (LINDELÖF MATH SOCIETY)

Math Club is a new club of CEBS, focused on events, talks, workshops, discussion and a space for opportunities in Mathematics at CEBS. Distinguished mathematicians are invited as speakers for talks (online and offline). Talks, competitions (Integration Bee, Hackathon) and workshops also take place within the student community at CEBS.

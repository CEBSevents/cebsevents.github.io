INTER IISER SPORTS MEET (IISM)

Budget Proposal — Academic Year 2026–27

The Sports Club has primarily two activities, viz. (i) IISM (Inter IISER Sports Meet), and (ii) Ragnarök. Inter IISER Sports Meet (IISM) is open to NISER, UM-DAE CEBS and IISc. Participation and achievements in sports from CEBS is increasing as expected from increasing strength of students in upcoming batches. Apart from IISM, CEBS students also participate in ICT Sports and Inter Departmental Sports Meet (Sportico) of University of Mumbai.

Ragnarök is a batch-wise sports meet organized in the even semester in which various sports events are organized to boost up the spirit of sportsmanship among students and to give them a fresh start for the semester. This event also serves as a prelude to IISM.

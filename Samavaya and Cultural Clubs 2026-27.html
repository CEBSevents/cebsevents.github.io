SAMAVĀYA and Cultural Clubs

Budget Proposal — Academic Year 2026–27

SAMAVĀYA – Annual Cultural Fest of CEBS is the last and most grand event to conclude the academic and cultural calendar of CEBS. It is hosted jointly by the student clubs of the institute consisting of both off–stage events which are held throughout the week in the Takshashila Mess and the Classrooms of the Prefabs and on–stage events. It is enjoyed by all the students, faculty, and administrative staff alike. The fest witnesses exceptional student participation every year and continues to be an enormous success.

SAMAVĀYA 2026 – the fourth edition of the annual cultural fest was celebrated with immense energy and enthusiasm from 31st March to 7th April 2026. True to its name – SamavĀya, meaning confluence – this fest was a remarkable coming together of all student-run cultural clubs of CEBS to create an unforgettable celebration of talent, creativity, and community.

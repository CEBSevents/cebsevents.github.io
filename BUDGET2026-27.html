1. INTER IISER NISER CEBS IISc CULTURAL MEET (IINCICM)

Budget Proposal — Academic Year 2026–27

The Inter-IISER NISER CEBS IISc Cultural Meet (IINCICM) is a national-level cultural and literary fest bringing together students from all IISERs, NISER, IISc, and CEBS. Hosted by a different institute each year, the event aims to provide a high-quality cultural experience. CEBS first participated in 2019 and has competed every year since 2022. Despite typically having a smaller contingent, our students have consistently won in many competitions. This year, the meet is expected to take place in IISc from 19th to 22nd December.

For administrative convenience, the IINCICM budget is managed under the CEBS Cultural Club, which allocates funds to various sub-clubs for their respective events in IINCICM. This section of the proposal covers the central logistics, travel, accommodation, club expenses and other management level expenses for the IICM contingent. The expenses have been estimated based on last year's charges by IISER Behrampur, the 2025 IINCICM host institute.

Budget Overview and Comparison

Taking into consideration previous years' approved budgets, the estimated total expenditure for this year's IINCICM contingent is ₹9,70,000/-.

2. INTER IISER SPORTS MEET (IISM)

Budget Proposal — Academic Year 2026–27

The Sports Club has primarily two activities, viz. (i) IISM (Inter IISER Sports Meet), and (ii) Ragnarök. Inter IISER Sports Meet (IISM) is open to NISER, UM-DAE CEBS and IISc. Participation and achievements in sports from CEBS is increasing as expected from increasing strength of students in upcoming batches. Apart from IISM, CEBS students also participate in ICT Sports and Inter Departmental Sports Meet (Sportico) of University of Mumbai.

Ragnarök is a batch-wise sports meet organized in the even semester in which various sports events are organized to boost up the spirit of sportsmanship among students and to give them a fresh start for the semester.

3. SAMAVĀYA and Cultural Clubs

Budget Proposal — Academic Year 2026–27

SAMAVĀYA – Annual Cultural Fest of CEBS is the last and most grand event to conclude the academic and cultural calendar of CEBS. It is hosted jointly by the student clubs of the institute consisting of both off–stage events which are held throughout the week in the Takshashila Mess and the Classrooms of the Prefabs and on–stage events. It is enjoyed by all the students, faculty, and administrative staff alike.

SAMAVĀYA 2026 – the fourth edition of the annual cultural fest was celebrated with immense energy and enthusiasm from 31st March to 7th April 2026. True to its name – SamavĀya, meaning confluence – this fest was a remarkable coming together of all student-run cultural clubs of CEBS to create an unforgettable celebration of talent, creativity, and community.

INTER IISER NISER CEBS IISc CULTURAL MEET (IINCICM)

Budget Proposal — Academic Year 2026–27

The Inter-IISER NISER CEBS IISc Cultural Meet (IINCICM) is a national-level cultural and literary fest bringing together students from all IISERs, NISER, IISc, and CEBS. Hosted by a different institute each year, the event aims to provide a high-quality cultural experience. CEBS first participated in 2019 and has competed every year since 2022. Despite typically having a smaller contingent, our students have consistently won in many competitions. This year, the meet is expected to take place in IISc from 19th to 22nd December.

For administrative convenience, the IINCICM budget is managed under the CEBS Cultural Club, which allocates funds to various sub-clubs for their respective events in IINCICM. This section of the proposal covers the central logistics, travel, accommodation, club expenses and other management level expenses for the IICM contingent. The expenses have been estimated based on last year's charges by IISER Behrampur, the 2025 IINCICM host institute.

Notes and Remarks

  • We are counting all the expenses for a contingent of 60 students, this number may vary. The expenses from IISc are not yet confirmed and we have projected previous year prices to achieve an estimate.
  • Registration fees may vary depending on the host institute (might go up or reduce).
  • The Dance and Theatre clubs require at least a month's worth of practice under the guidance of a coach. The Coach will charge per session according to institute norms. They will choreograph Synchro, Group dance and Classical Dance for the contingent.
  • The Art club requires funds for five costumes in the Fashion Design competition. Some materials will be used for practice which are included in the Canvas painting section. Canvas painting will also require a purchase of practice canvases before the event.
  • Short film expenses will be done before IINCICM, as we need to submit a complete film.
  • The costumes for Dance and Theatre will be rented from Mumbai and will be returned to the vendor after the contingent returns.
